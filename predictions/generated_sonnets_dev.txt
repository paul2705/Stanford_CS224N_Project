So, now I have confessed that he is thine
And I myself am mortgaged to thy will,
Myself I'll forfeit, so that other mine
As a prize may blind love fix.
Against his words he stalks like a wolf from me
Like half a dove unseen singing though asleep,
Even now after this mode brave folk so hear
Empire's foe's might screaming right over.
All this till hate vexes him, leaves his tale missed
And poor gain none despite being begged
Yet filled with chatterers hind of duty to suspect.
  On spite of fate's prime injury,
  His lie continues thither till heart's end days grow old.

In faith, I do not love thee with mine eyes,
For they in thee a thousand errors note;
But 'tis my heart that loves what they despise,
As bright in thine inward, bitter of any reproach.
My trust is told that earthly sight surplasheth thee;
On earth love aloof love, which, plagues to cast
And fear to fill hold in these forebodings,
Since, my love's heart liveth in thine eyes straight:
'T is happier therefore that I lie 't by thy honest sight,
Than being attacked while in thy blunder you lie.
  But trust thou so in my night of mine eye,

Love is my sin, and thy dear virtue hate,
Hate of my sin, grounded on sinful loving.
O, but with mine compare thou thine own state,
Hate my extreme in praise for thy beauty!
Sorely confounded, the sight those gems having been,
Accept into my treasure: my blessed health
Be damned that fellt beauty's elegant defect,
For grace may plead evil away against me.
  I hate thy love, sad, bereft me with the shame!
